[8] prose (12 lines)
    Anyway, I'm finally getting into some of these Yoshi's '98 shows.
    This is a great solo gig w/ some minor accompaniment by Steve
    Kimock.  While Kimock is certainly audible, he chooses to lay low
    with his solos (IMO of course).  Bruce, on the other hand, is on
    fire!  We get lots of extended piano jams (Country Doctor, Way It Is
    are both very long) mixed with a great setlist.
    A few other things to mention...
    The Terrapin Jam that starts the late show is not the traditional
    one that we know from Bruce...it is actually the closing jam of
    Terrapin that he brilliantly weaves into a unique version of Way It
    Is.  We then get the more familiar Terp jam in the middle of Way It
    Is.  I could go on and on, but I'll just stop here and let the music
